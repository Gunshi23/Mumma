A Letter from my Heart

Dear Mom,

There aren't enough words in any language to express the depth of my gratitude for everything you have done. From the sleepless nights of my childhood to the unwavering support you offer me today, you have been my constant North Star.

Your strength is my inspiration, your kindness is my map, and your love is my greatest treasure. You've taught me how to be brave, how to be gentle, and how to see the beauty in even the simplest things.

I hope this little corner of the internet brings a smile to your face, just as you have brought countless smiles to mine. You deserve the world today, and every other day of the year.

With all my love,

Your Loving Child ❤️
